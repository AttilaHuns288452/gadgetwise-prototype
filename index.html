How GadgetWise chooses

Your answers are compared against the specifications and ownership data in the GadgetWise catalog. Products that fail your must-haves are filtered out first. The rest are scored on budget fit, academic suitability, performance, battery, portability, durability, repairability, warranty, student rating and long-term value — weighted by what you told us.
