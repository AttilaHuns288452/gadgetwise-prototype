How GadgetWise chooses

We check your answers against each product’s specs and ownership cost. Products that miss your must-haves are filtered out first. The rest are scored on budget fit, academic suitability, performance, battery, portability, durability, repairability, warranty, student rating and long-term value, weighted by what you told us. No AI is used to choose the product.
